===============================================================================
 FIREFIGHT REINFORCEMENTS - KNOWN ISSUES
 Written 2026-11-23, after the round-4 fix pass.
 Everything below is a real open risk. Nothing here is hypothetical padding.
===============================================================================

 WHAT THE ROUND-4 PASS CLOSED, so you do not go looking for it here:
   * Courtyard's Spartan purchase delivering both bodies on a different storey.
   * The 10,000 MAC STRIKE deducting the points before testing whether the
     match was already ending. All seven "ff" maps.
   * The buyer test accepting a player at exactly 0 health.
   * Unearthed's six-Marine purchase splitting two bodies ~95 world units from
     the other four.
 Each of those four was verified by decompiling the shipped file and reading
 the changed path back out of its own bytes, and by a byte-level diff in which
 every differing byte falls inside an intended field.

1. NOTHING HAS EVER RUN IN RETAIL MCC.
   Not one map, not one purchase, not one install, not on any machine. Every
   claim made for this package is static: an independent reader of the cache
   header, an independent checksum, Blamite (the cache library inside the
   Assembly modding tool) loading each file, and a decompile of each map's own
   script table. None of that is the game. Treat a crash as normal.

2. WHETHER MCC LOADS A REPLACED BUILT-IN FIREFIGHT CACHE IS UNCONFIRMED.
   Seven of the eight maps are rebuilt copies of Firefight maps that ship with
   the game. The normal install registers them through MCC's mod manifest
   rather than overwriting the game's files. No source - not 343's
   documentation, not the modding community, not any bug report we could find -
   states whether MCC will actually load a mod-supplied cache in place of a
   built-in Firefight map. It may silently use the stock map instead. This is
   the single biggest unknown in the package and it is unresolved.

3. THE SEVEN "ff" MAPS CARRY A STALE RETAIL SIGNATURE. UNPROVEN EITHER WAY.
   The MCC cache header holds a content-hash mask, a security salt, three
   SHA-1 content hashes, an RSA key-blob hash and a 256-byte RSA signature.
   In all seven rebuilt maps those fields are byte-identical to the retail
   originals, while the content they cover has changed. Assembly does not model
   or recompute them, so every Assembly-made Reach MCC map mod has this
   property - which is the practical argument that MCC tolerates it.
   Against that: the mod author's own working ff_unearthed.map, which has been
   played, has its RSA signature and RSA key-blob hash zeroed rather than left
   stale. That suggests the official toolset deliberately clears them. We could
   not find any published account of what MCC actually checks. If a map is
   rejected at load, zeroing bytes 0x3AC-0x4CB of the header and recomputing
   the checksum at 0x360 is the first thing to try.
   (The two Unearthed builds inherit the author's zeroed signature already.)

4. ALLY SPAWN WALKABILITY IS INFERRED, NOT MEASURED.
   Where an added ally spawn point stands is taken from that map's own player
   starting locations - positions the engine stands a Spartan up on at round
   start. That is the strongest evidence available without running the game,
   and it is still an inference. The BSP collision data was never read. No
   spawn point in this package has been confirmed to sit on walkable floor by
   querying the map's geometry. An ally could spawn stuck or fall.
   Round 4 narrowed this but did not close it. Every ally spawn position on
   COURTYARD is now one of Courtyard's own player starting locations; the two
   spawn points round 2 added to each of Waterfront's and Outpost's trooper
   squads likewise. Measured against each map's own respawn cluster, the ally
   spawn points still further than 12 world units out, all of them inside the
   safe zone and in no kill volume, are:
     * GLACIER     28.9 units, 1.8 units below its siblings. One point of four
                   on the two trooper squads. This is the largest isolation
                   left in the package: at most one trooper in four goes there
                   and the other three arrive correctly.
     * OUTPOST     15.7 units. One point of four on a trooper squad, and one
                   of the two points its Spartan squad has.
     * WATERFRONT  15.8 and 13.5 units - and these are the ONLY two points one
                   of its two Spartan squads has, so that squad's purchase has
                   no nearer point to fall back on. Plus 14.4 units on one
                   trooper point of four and one Spartan point of two.
     * OVERLOOK    15.0 units. One point of four on a trooper squad and one of
                   the two points its Spartan squad has.
     * BEACHHEAD   13.1 units. One point of four on each trooper squad.
   None of those was touched in round 4, on purpose: the brief for this pass
   was Courtyard, and all of these have vertical offsets under 1.9 units, so
   none shows the different-storey signature - 2.6 units of drop across 1.2
   units of ground - that made Courtyard's pair the one worth the risk of
   another write. If a Spartan purchase lands away from the shop in play, the
   likeliest map is WATERFRONT and the likeliest cause is this. A body that
   arrives far from the shop still arrives inside the arena.
   UNEARTHED is a separate case and weaker: the containment test does not
   calibrate on that map at all - none of its 16 player starting locations
   register inside its safe zone, and neither do the four ORIGINAL Marine
   spawn points - so no safe-zone or kill-volume claim is made for Unearthed in
   either direction. Its coordinates rest on provenance alone: every one of
   them is a spawn position the map itself already authors.

5. ff_unearthed.tuned.map IS THE SAFEST ARTIFACT IN THE PACKAGE.
   It is the only build whose script table was never recompiled: it differs
   from the author's own played map in four bytes and zero in size. The seven
   "ff" maps, by contrast, had their entire scenario script table regenerated,
   including roughly 360 of Bungie's own scripts per map - structurally clean,
   semantically unverified, and by far the largest blast radius here. If the
   six-Marine Unearthed build (ff_unearthed.map in Firefight-Reinforcements)
   misbehaves, the Safe folder's copy is tuned.map and is the fallback.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 SMALLER OPEN ITEMS, FOR COMPLETENESS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6. The installer and the revert script have NEVER BEEN EXECUTED. They were
   written for Windows PowerShell; there is no PowerShell on the machine that
   built this package, so both were reviewed by reading them and in no other
   way. Run UNINSTALL.bat with no arguments first - that is a dry run and
   changes nothing - and read its plan before you ever pass -Apply.

7. On-screen shop price text is frozen. The string tables are SHA-1 checked in
   the header and were deliberately left untouched, so a prompt may quote an
   old number even when the real cost has changed. The charge is correct; the
   text is not.

8. Both Unearthed builds declare the same scenario path,
   levels\dlc\ff_unearthed\ff_unearthed, because their own map headers say so.
   Their map GUIDs are now distinct, but whether MCC shows two separate entries
   for one scenario path is untested. See INSTALL.txt if Unearthed appears once
   or appears wrong.

9. ff_unearthed.marines6.map has a debug-section virtual address that neither a
   retail file nor the author's own map uses: the growth was absorbed into the
   section offset mask instead of advancing the address. The arithmetic is
   self-consistent, the file tiles exactly and Blamite reads it, and retail
   files carry a larger overlap of the same kind - so this is a low-probability
   risk, not a defect. It is another reason to keep tuned.map as the fallback.

10. Both Unearthed builds have two resource pages whose extent runs past the end
    of the resource segment. This condition is present, unchanged, in the
    author's own played map, so it is inherited and not introduced here. All
    seven "ff" maps have none.

11. Firefight Corvette is deliberately not in this package. It places no
    Firefight respawn markers, so there is no coordinate in it known to be
    floor an ally could spawn on.

12. THE LONGSWORD IS LEAKED RATHER THAN DESTROYED. Six of the seven "ff" maps
    (not Glacier, which has no Longsword at all). The LONGSWORD PASS creates
    the map's Longsword device, flies it and never destroys it, so the model
    can sit parked over the arena until the map's own weapon-resupply cycle
    re-animates and clears it - an interval nothing here bounds. This was a
    deliberate choice and it is being kept: the previous behaviour destroyed
    the device, and that could leave the map's own resupply event waiting
    forever on a device that no longer exists, which is unrecoverable. A
    visible aeroplane is better than a dead event. Purely cosmetic.

13. WHAT THE ROUND-4 EDITS PHYSICALLY ARE, for anyone auditing this next.
    Courtyard's and Unearthed's spawn fixes are in-place overwrites of 12-byte
    position fields - six fields and two fields respectively - with no block
    allocated, no count changed, no pointer changed and no change in file size.
    The script fixes on the seven "ff" maps are a normal script-table rewrite:
    script count and global count are unchanged on every map, so no script
    object was added, and the expression count rose by exactly 8 per map.
    Every one of the nine shipped caches re-parses with zero warnings, stores
    a header checksum equal to the recomputed one, and is accepted by Blamite.
    Waterfront and Outpost were rebuilt from build/r2/lin/<map>.tagfix.map, the
    only base that carries their four-spawn-point trooper fix together with an
    unwritten script table; building them from anything else silently regresses
    those squads to two spawn points. See project/build/r2/README-COORDINATION.txt.
